Why one extra power of pp survives

The Type I estimate in Alpöge’s construction leaves room for extra torsion. The paper shows that the arithmetic actually uses some of that room. For each sufficiently large member of an original family and each word in that member, it selects a prime pp and a place ww above it. One class in the original word quotient has exact additive order pT+1p^{T+1}, and its image at ww has that same order. Here TT is the cutoff for the chosen fibre. The production corollary supplies a member and word beyond any prescribed bound. Exact statement and hypotheses

Glossary

  • Word. A piece of the source indexing data that determines the global lattice quotient being studied. The proof fixes it before choosing a prime.
  • Served place. A place ww above pp where the source supplies the comparison for that word.
  • Lattice. A module of integral vectors inside a vector space over a fraction field. The local lattices here use the coefficient ring OwO_w.
  • Torsion. A class annihilated by some positive integer. Its additive order is the least positive integer that annihilates it.
  • Frobenius. In the calculation modulo pp, the map raising coordinates to their ppth powers. It relates the constant frames of consecutive slots.
  • Cutoff. The exponent TT that determines the scalar layer pTAwp^TA_w in the local denominator. The proof detects survival inside this layer.

The idea

A quotient forgets whatever lies in its denominator. To prove that a vector survives, find a measurement that vanishes on the denominator but takes a nonzero value on the vector. Here the measurement is an integral linear functional, called a row, used modulo pT+1p^{T+1}. It takes a unit value on the image of a ramified form. Multiplying that form by pTp^T gives a reading of pTp^T times a unit, which is still nonzero modulo pT+1p^{T+1}.

The hard part is making this measurement on the same word and place where the source comparison and denominator are defined. The final step needs a global killing power as well as local survival. These are two different pieces of the proof.

How to find the separating row

Work back from the first missing layer. If the scalar cutoff is TT, a row that reads a unit on a numerator awa_w will read a nonzero value on pTawp^Ta_w modulo pT+1p^{T+1}. The literal denominator is indexed by subsets of orbits. Choose one orbit and ask for two bounds. A term omitting it needs T+1T+1 powers of pp from its scalar, while a term retaining it needs those powers from the row on its neighbour operator. This gives the prime search a target. Look among eligible steps for a pair of top positions with enough factors and enough omission depth. Counting pairs in the fixed member measures this directly. The production window supplies a member and word to which that search applies. Selection and depth · Denominator · Production

Turn the edge relation into a coefficient. Modulo pp, the normalized incoming line and hyperplane are orthogonal at each slot. Across the next slot the constant frames have been raised to the ppth power. Contracting the ppth powers of the first vector's coordinates with the second vector restores the orthogonality identity even when the two flags vary independently. To realize the powers in the labelled coefficients, take pp copies of the same flag within the first slot. Each disjoint contraction supplies one power of pp. To see how the row can read a unit on the output, keep the standard hyperplane and shear the line from e1e_1 to e1+eke_1+e_k. The last dual coordinate then reads 11. Frobenius contraction and output unit

Move the test to a word class. To get a unit on an actual global form, it suffices that the compared ramified numerators span the chart lattice. If every such numerator read into the maximal ideal, the entire span would read there. The integral masking and sign differences supply this spanning family. Once the row gives local survival and the denominator gives eventual vanishing, look for the first crossing between them. The multiplier supplied there clears the global denominator, and the preceding local power can only vanish if a second multiplier contradicts minimality. Ramified seed · Exact order
